Hidn — Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

Hidn is a small app made by Amaury and Ebba Gouverneur Regnström for sharing travel tips with friends. This page says what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and how you sign in (Apple, Google, or email and password); the name, @handle, profile photo and bio you choose.
  • What you share: tips (text, photos, the place), comments, likes, saves, tags, trips and future trips, your friendships, and messages you send to friends.
  • Your location — only on your phone. The map uses it to show tips near you. We don't store your location; the app only asks the server for tips inside the area shown on your screen.
  • Photos you choose. They're resized on your phone before upload, which removes their location and other metadata. If a photo knows where it was taken, the app uses that on your phone to suggest the tip's place; the location itself isn't sent or stored.

We don't collect contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics, we don't sell or rent anything, and there are no ads.

Who can see what

  • Your friends (friendships are always mutual) see your tips with your name, your trips, and who you are in comments, likes and tags.
  • Friends of your friends can find your tips on the map without your name, or — if you choose "Don't show to friends of friends" on a tip — only that someone recommends the place.
  • Saving: everyone who can read a tip sees how many people saved it; its author sees who — by name if you're friends, otherwise as "a friend of" your friends in common.
  • Your saved tips and future trips are private unless you invite friends to a future trip.
  • Messages are seen only by you and the friend you write to. You can write only to friends. A tip you send shows to them as Hidn would show it anyway: without the author's name if they aren't the author's friend, and not at all if the author kept it for their own friends.
  • Your profile (name, @handle, photo, bio) can be seen by signed-in Hidn users who look you up, so friends can find you.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase stores the app's data and photos (servers in the EU, Paris).
  • Apple and Google, if you sign in with them.
  • Apple Maps looks up places and addresses, from your phone.

Keeping and deleting

Your data is kept until you delete it. Delete a tip from its page: its photos are deleted with it. Delete your whole account in Settings → Delete account: your profile and profile photo, login, tips and their photos, comments, likes, saves, trips, friendships, messages and notices are deleted. Places you added stay on the map for others, without your name. Photos you take out of a tip, or add to a draft you don't post within 30 days, are deleted too.

Children

Hidn isn't meant for children under 13.
